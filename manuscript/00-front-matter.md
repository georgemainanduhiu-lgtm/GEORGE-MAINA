# Before Kickoff: A Note to You {.unnumbered}

Picture a stadium with eighty thousand people in it.

The noise is so loud you can feel it in your chest. Flags are waving. Drums are booming. Somewhere in that huge crowd, a little kid is wearing a shirt with a famous name on the back. Down on the grass, the player with that name is about to take a penalty kick. The whole world is watching.

It looks like magic, doesn't it? It looks like that player was *born* to be there.

But here's a secret that most people never hear: **every single superstar in this book was once a kid who got told "no."**

One was told he was too small to ever play professional soccer. Another had to run to a bomb shelter when he was six years old. One rode as many as five buses, four hours each way, just to get to practice. One missed a penalty in the biggest game of his life and had to face millions of people afterward. One got injured so many times that people called him a waste of money. One played sixteen games for his first team without scoring a single goal. And that was the boy who would grow up to break scoring records!

These players didn't become heroes because everything went right. They became heroes because of what they did when things went *wrong*.

That's what this book is about.

Inside, you'll meet twelve soccer stars from all over the world: from Argentina and Portugal, Croatia and Egypt, France and Brazil, Norway and England, Turkey and Spain. Some grew up in tiny villages. Some grew up in big, busy cities. Some grew up with very little money. They speak different languages, play in different positions, and cheer for different teams.

But they all share three things:

1. **Courage.** They kept going when they were scared, hurt, or sad.
2. **Teamwork.** They learned that nobody wins alone, not even the best player on the planet.
3. **Big dreams.** They believed in something bigger than what they could see right in front of them.

## How to Use This Book

Every chapter tells the true story of one player. You can read the chapters in order, or you can jump straight to your favorite player. (We won't tell anyone!)

Each chapter has:

- **A Fact File**, so you can quickly learn who the player is.
- **The Story**: the ups, the downs, and the never-give-up moments.
- **A Never-Give-Up Lesson**: the big idea you can use in your own life, on the field or off it.
- **Fun Facts**: cool things to share with your friends.
- **Your Turn**: questions and challenges to think about.

At the end of the book, you'll find a quiz to test your super-fan knowledge, a list of soccer words, and something we call **the Never-Give-Up Playbook**.

One more thing before we kick off. Look for the ways these stories connect. Heroes from different chapters will bump into each other again and again: as teammates, as rivals, and sometimes in the very same final. Soccer is a giant story, and every player is one part of it.

And so are you.

Ready? Let's go!
