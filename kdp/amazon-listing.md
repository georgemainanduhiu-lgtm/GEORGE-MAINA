## Pricing and programs

- **Kindle eBook:** $3.99 to $4.99. This stays in the 70% royalty band ($2.99 to $9.99).
- **KDP Select:** Enroll for the first 90 days. You'll be in Kindle Unlimited, where many parents borrow kids' books, and you'll get promotion tools (Kindle Countdown Deals).
- **Paperback:** Strongly recommended. Many buyers of children's books want a physical gift. At ~30,000 words, expect roughly 140 to 170 pages at 6 × 9 inches. Price it around $9.99 to $12.99. The included DOCX is a good starting point for the print interior. Set 0.75" margins and add page numbers in Word, then export a PDF.
- **Launch timing:** October and November are ideal, because holiday gift shopping peaks in late November and December.

## Cover guidance

The included `dist/cover.jpg` is a clean **placeholder**: typography on a soccer field. To sell well, consider a professional illustrated cover. Brief for a designer:

- **Size:** 2,560 × 1,600 px (height × width), JPG, RGB.
- **Style:** Bright, energetic, cartoon or illustrated. A diverse group of kid soccer players in generic kits, or dynamic action silhouettes.
- **Do NOT use:** real player photos (you need a license), recognizable likenesses, club crests, FIFA or World Cup logos, or national federation badges. These cause trademark and right-of-publicity problems, and KDP may block the book.
- **Must be readable as a thumbnail:** big title, high contrast, and "Ages 8–12" visible.

## Selling tips

1. **Get early reviews.** Ask friends, parents, teachers, and coaches who read it to leave honest reviews. Never pay for reviews; it breaks Amazon's rules.
2. **Use the free Look Inside.** The first chapter (Messi) is a strong hook, so keep it at the front.
3. **Update yearly.** Soccer changes fast. A "2027 Edition" with refreshed stats can relaunch the book.
4. **Build a series.** Possible follow-ups: *Women's Soccer Heroes Who Never Gave Up*, *Basketball Heroes Who Never Gave Up*, *World Cup Legends*.
5. **Amazon Ads:** Start with a small daily budget ($5 to $10) on automatic targeting, then switch to manual keywords that convert.
